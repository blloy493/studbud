StudBud

A Chrome extension that breaks your assignment into small steps and walks you through them until it's done.

Add to Chrome

What StudBud does

Upload or paste your assignment. StudBud turns it into one clear step at a time, with a short note on how to approach each one. It organizes the work and explains what each question is asking. It does not do the work for you.

  1. Upload a PDF or image of the assignment, or paste the text.
  2. Answer a couple of quick questions if StudBud needs more detail.
  3. Follow the steps. An on-screen avatar gives you hints when you're stuck.

You get 3 free assignment breakdowns to try it. After that, you can buy a pass.
