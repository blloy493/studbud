How StudBud helps

  • One step at a time. You see only the step you're on, so you always know what to do next.
  • A note on how to approach each step. StudBud explains what each question is asking. It does not write the answer.
  • Help when you're stuck. The on-screen avatar gives hints that get more detailed if you still need them.
  • No switching tabs. The steps and the avatar sit on the page you're already working on.
  • Optional focus timer. A 25-minute timer for each step, and an avatar that levels up as you finish steps.

StudBud can miss parts of complicated PDFs, such as tables, graphs and long structured prompts. Skim the steps against your assignment sheet before you start.

How to install StudBud

Install video coming soon
  1. Use Chrome on a computer. Windows, Mac, Linux and Chromebook work. Phones and tablets can't install Chrome extensions.
  2. Add it from the Chrome Web Store. Open the StudBud page, click "Add to Chrome", then "Add extension" in the pop-up.
  3. Pin StudBud (optional). Click the puzzle-piece icon in the Chrome toolbar, then the pin next to StudBud. This keeps its settings one click away.
  4. Open your assignment. Open or refresh the page you're working on. The avatar appears at the bottom left and the step widget at the bottom right. Chrome blocks extensions on the Chrome Web Store and chrome:// pages, so StudBud won't show there.
  5. Add your assignment. Name your avatar, then upload a PDF, PNG or JPEG (up to 3 MB) or paste the text. Answer any quick questions, then follow the steps.

About Chrome's permission pop-up. Chrome will say StudBud can "read and change all your data on all websites". StudBud needs this only to show the avatar and step widget on whatever page you're studying on. It does not read page content, form fields or your browsing history. You can turn StudBud off on any site from its toolbar popup. Details are in the privacy policy.
